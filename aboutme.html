ABOUT ME

Allie Hopper (also known as Bug) is a designer based in the American Midwest. Allie uses they/them pronouns, and loves The Matrix (no connection between those two facts, I’m sure). They graduated from the renowned DAAP program at the University of Cincinnati in 2023. During this time, they worked across a wide swath of the design industry, including an internship at Hasbro Inc. Allie has continued their art education since graduation, as of late tackling pottery and sewing.

Allie Bug’s art prioritizes color and texture across a multitude of mediums. It leans into nostalgia, sentimentality, warm feelings, and celebration of here and now and every moment. On this site you’ll see a variety of digital painting, card and letter design, paper-making, felt-work, crochet, sketch work, to name just a few. Thanks for visiting this site, and taking a peek into Allie’s world!
